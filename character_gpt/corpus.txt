The small machine learns one character at a time.
It reads a little book, remembers patterns, and tries to write a new line.
A quiet workshop fills with words, questions, and patient experiments.

A model begins with numbers. The numbers become useful when examples show them
which symbols tend to follow other symbols. Attention lets each position look
back at the context before it chooses what comes next.

This tiny corpus is deliberately plain. It is large enough to demonstrate
training and generation, yet small enough to run on a laptop while learning.
The best experiments are easy to repeat: change the text, run the script, and
compare the sample produced by the model.

Curiosity starts the process. Careful measurement improves it. Each pass through
the data adjusts the model a little, and each generated character reveals what
it has learned so far.
